# Trace Evidence & Digital Forensics

Unified into one unit in the UGC NET FS Paper II syllabus, though they're methodologically different disciplines.

## Trace evidence

**Hair.** Morphology (cuticle, cortex, medulla), colour, length, root vs shaft. Increasingly supplanted by mitochondrial DNA typing for reliable individualisation.

**Fibres.** Natural (cotton, wool, silk) vs synthetic (nylon, polyester, acrylic). Microscopy for morphology, FTIR/Raman for polymer identification, MSP (microspectrophotometry) for colour.

**Glass.** Refractive index measurement (GRIM), density, elemental profile by LA-ICP-MS. Direction of impact from radial and concentric fracture patterns.

**Paint.** Layer structure by cross-section microscopy, FTIR per layer, pyrolysis-GC-MS for polymer type. Hit-and-run cases live and die on paint transfer.

## Digital forensics

**Acquisition.** Bit-for-bit forensic image of the source (hard drive, phone, USB). Cryptographic hash (MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256) taken before and after to prove integrity.

**Chain of custody.** Documented for digital evidence exactly as for physical — who had the drive, when, why.

**Common artifacts.** Browser history, deleted-file recovery from unallocated space, EXIF metadata in images, call detail records, cell-tower data.

**Mobile forensics.** Logical vs physical acquisition; JTAG and chip-off for damaged devices.

**Anti-forensics.** Encryption, secure delete, VMs, live boot. Investigators counter with volatile memory capture, network taps, warrant-backed decryption assistance.
