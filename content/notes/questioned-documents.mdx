# Questioned Documents

Disciplines that examine any document whose authenticity, authorship, or integrity is in dispute.

## Handwriting analysis

Based on the assumption that no two people write identically (individual characteristics) *and* no one writes exactly the same way twice (natural variation).

**Individual characteristics:** slant, letter shape, spacing, connections, pen lifts, pressure patterns.

**Comparison workflow:**

1. Establish authorship of known standards (contemporaneous, same medium, comparable content).
2. Extract individual and class characteristics.
3. Compare questioned document against knowns.
4. Reach conclusion — identification, elimination, or inconclusive.

## Ink and paper examination

**Ink:** TLC, HPTLC, video spectral comparator (VSC), FTIR. Dating by dye migration and solvent loss is possible but contested.

**Paper:** watermark identification, fibre composition, optical brightener response under UV.

## Alteration detection

- **Erasures** — chemical or mechanical; visible under oblique light, UV, IR.
- **Additions** — different ink or writing instrument via VSC.
- **Indented writing** — recovered non-destructively using **ESDA** (Electrostatic Detection Apparatus).

## Printed / photocopied documents

Printer class and individual marks (banding, drum defects), toner composition by FTIR/pyrolysis-GC-MS, staple marks, folding.

> **Draft content** — signature forgery categories (traced, freehand, simulated) to be expanded.
